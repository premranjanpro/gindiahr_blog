India's GCC Talent Boom: The Skills Employers Will Chase Next

By GIndiaHR Insights · 6 min read
← Back to Insights

India's Global Capability Centres are increasingly moving beyond cost-focused operations into technology, analytics, product, finance and strategic functions. That changes the talent requirement: employers need people who combine technical depth with business understanding.

High-demand capability areas

  • Generative AI and applied AI engineering
  • Cloud architecture and platform engineering
  • Data engineering and analytics
  • Cybersecurity and risk
  • Product management and domain expertise
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management

The real shortage is hybrid talent

A company may find a developer who knows a framework, but the harder profile is someone who understands technology, customer outcomes and the business domain. GCC hiring is therefore becoming more selective around problem-solving and ownership.

What employers can change

Build talent pipelines earlier, partner with specialist recruiters for scarce skills, create internal upskilling paths and use structured assessments for technical and business capability.

India's next GCC advantage will be measured by capability, not just headcount.

Bottom line: organisations that combine hiring with reskilling will have a better chance of building durable GCC teams than those relying only on lateral hiring.
